# Global Purchasing Solutions LLC · GGG Platforms · GGG TechLab

**GGG TechLab builds the Web3, fintech, crypto, trade and lifestyle technology behind the GGG Ecosystem.**

Global Purchasing Solutions LLC is a Delaware company that operates GGG Platforms and the GGG Ecosystem, a group of more than 80 domains in global trade, commodities, technology, real estate, luxury and lifestyle services. GGG TechLab is the group's engineering arm. It designs, builds and runs the software behind every GGG brand, and publishes selected tools here as open source.

## What we build

- **Web3 websites and applications.** Modern web and mobile products, ready to connect to blockchain networks and digital wallets.
- **Fintech systems.** Invoicing, payments, accounting and financial-control tools for businesses that trade across borders.
- **Crypto platforms.** Digital-asset, smart-contract and wallet infrastructure.
- **Trade platforms.** Global purchasing and commodities trade desks for metals, energy, food and general trade.
- **Lifestyle management systems.** Booking and concierge platforms for luxury, yachts, travel and premium services.
- **AI and automation.** The GGG V25 Master Intelligence System coordinates 29 business divisions with AI agents. A person approves every message, post and payment before it leaves the system.
